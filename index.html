How Rootly works

Three simple steps.
Smarter plant care.

Rootly is designed as a retrofit kit that brings moisture-aware watering to your existing plant pots.

01

Sense

A soil-moisture sensor checks the moisture level around your plant's roots.

02

Respond

When the soil needs water, the controller can activate a small pump to deliver water through a tube.

03

Stay connected

A companion app concept lets you monitor moisture, control watering and receive useful alerts.

A few quick answers

Rootly, clarified.

Does Rootly require a new smart pot?

No. Rootly is designed as a retrofit kit that can be used with existing pots, subject to final compatibility testing.

Is the product available to buy today?

No. Rootly is currently a concept-stage prototype project. The hardware and app experience are under development and require further testing.

Does this website connect to a real sensor?

No. The dashboard above is an interactive simulation for demonstration. It uses sample values and does not control a pump or read live sensor data.

What is the goal of Rootly?

To make plant care easier and more water-conscious through affordable moisture monitoring and automated watering technology.
